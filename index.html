Graduation Project

SUNSHIELD

A Deep Learning-Based Framework for Anomaly Detection in PV Systems

A forecasting-based system that predicts expected photovoltaic behaviour from solar irradiance and temperature data, then flags anomalies when readings deviate beyond a 3-sigma threshold.

  • Benchmarks four sequence architectures: LSTM, BiLSTM, GRU and Transformer.
  • Learns normal system behaviour from irradiance and temperature time series.
  • Uses the forecast residual with a 3σ rule to separate faults from noise.
  • LSTM
  • BiLSTM
  • GRU
  • Transformer
  • Time Series
